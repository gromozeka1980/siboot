Wiki is a Ripi male, just as Siboot was, 33 years old.  He is a happy-go-lucky fellow, always ready with a wry joke.  His talent for making puns across several languages strikes terror in the hearts of all who must endure his creations.  He has a strong sense of obligation to those who help him and a lot of personal integrity, although he finds it hard to have faith in other people.  He is not vain but he does have a bit of a temper.
