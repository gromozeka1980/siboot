Gardbore is a Frem male, 43 years old.  He has been waiting for his chance to compete for the Shepherdship since Feslym became the Fourth Shepherd.  The years of waiting have not sat well with Gardbore; he is aloof and cynical.  A proud and intelligent person, Gardbore looks down on the other acolytes.  He really doesn't care what they think about him.  He is not very trustworthy, but he is loyal to those he considers his friends.  He is also very secretive.
